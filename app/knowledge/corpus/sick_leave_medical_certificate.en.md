# Sick Leave and Medical Certificates

## KSA Sick Leave Tiers
In Saudi Arabia, sick leave within a rolling year is paid in tiers: the first 30 days at full pay,
the next 60 days at 75% pay, and a final 30 days unpaid (Saudi Labour Law Art. 117). Other
countries in this group do not yet have a documented tiered structure -- contact HR directly for
sick leave pay questions outside KSA.

## Medical Certificate Requirement
A medical certificate from a licensed clinic or hospital is required for any sick leave of more
than one day, and may be requested for a single day at your manager's discretion.

## Backdated Sick Leave
Sick leave reported after the fact is accepted within a reasonable window (a few days) provided a
medical certificate covering the dates in question is submitted alongside the request.
