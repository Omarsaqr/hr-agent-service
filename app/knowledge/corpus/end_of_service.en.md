# End-of-Service Benefit

## What It Is
Employees in Saudi Arabia are entitled to an end-of-service benefit (gratuity) when their
employment ends: half a month's wage per year for the first five years of service, and a full
month's wage per year after that.

## Resignation Reduces the Amount
If you resign rather than being terminated, the benefit is reduced on a sliding scale: nothing
under 2 years of service, one-third of the full amount between 2 and 5 years, two-thirds between 5
and 10 years, and the full amount at 10 years or more.

## Requesting a Calculation
Ask HR for an end-of-service estimate at any time; the calculation is based on your final basic
wage and exact years and months of service, not a rounded figure.
