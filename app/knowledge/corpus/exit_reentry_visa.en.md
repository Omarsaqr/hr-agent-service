# Exit/Re-Entry Visa

## Who Needs One
Non-Saudi employees residing in KSA on an Iqama must obtain an exit/re-entry visa before leaving
the country if they intend to return and continue employment. Without one, re-entry is not
possible on the same Iqama.

## Requesting One
Submit the request through the HR portal at least 5 working days before travel, stating your
planned departure and return dates. HR processes exit/re-entry visas through the Muqeem platform
on your behalf; you do not need a separate government appointment.

## Single vs. Multiple Exit/Re-Entry
A single exit/re-entry visa covers one trip. Employees who travel frequently for business can
request a multiple exit/re-entry visa, valid for repeated travel within a set period, subject to
manager approval.
