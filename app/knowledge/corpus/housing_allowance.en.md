# Housing Allowance

## Eligibility
Employees not provided company housing receive a monthly housing allowance as part of their
compensation package. The amount is set in your employment contract and varies by grade and
location.

## Payment
The housing allowance is paid monthly alongside base salary, not as a separate lump sum, unless
your contract specifies an annual payment -- check your offer letter if you are unsure which
applies to you.

## Changing Your Housing Arrangement
If you move from company-provided housing to your own accommodation (or vice versa), notify HR
within 30 days so your compensation package can be adjusted accordingly.
