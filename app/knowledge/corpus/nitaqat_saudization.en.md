# Nitaqat and Saudization

## What Nitaqat Is
Nitaqat is the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources program that rates companies by the proportion of
Saudi nationals in their workforce, sorted into color-coded bands from Platinum down to Red.

## Why It Matters to You
A company's Nitaqat band affects its ability to renew existing employees' Iqamas and issue new work
visas. HR tracks the company's Saudization ratio continuously so hiring and renewals stay
compliant.

## Employee Questions
If you are asked to provide documentation for a Saudization audit (such as confirming your job
title matches your registered occupation), please respond promptly -- delays can affect the whole
company's band.
