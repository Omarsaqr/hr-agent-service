# Annual Leave Policy by Country

## Saudi Arabia
21 days per year, rising to 30 days after 5 years of continuous service (Saudi Labour Law Art.
109). The exact figures for your case are computed from your hire date -- use get_leave_balance
rather than this document for your personal entitlement.

## UAE
30 calendar days per year after one year of service; a partial accrual of 2 days per month applies
between 6 and 12 months of service.

## Egypt
15 days in the first year of service, rising to 21 days from the second year, and 30 days after 10
years of service or at age 50.

## Jordan
14 days per year, rising to 21 days after 5 years with the same employer.

## Requesting Leave
Regardless of country, all annual leave requests go through preview_leave_request and
submit_leave_request so the request is checked against your actual current balance before it is
sent for approval.
