# Salary Certificate

## What It Is
A salary certificate is an official letter confirming your job title, monthly salary, and length
of service. Banks, embassies, and landlords commonly request one.

## How to Request One
Request a salary certificate through the HR portal. Specify whether you need it addressed to a
specific bank or authority, since some require particular wording. Certificates are issued in
English or Arabic on request.

## Turnaround Time
Standard certificates are issued within 3 working days. Certificates requiring translation or
embassy attestation take longer -- HR will advise a specific date when you submit the request.
