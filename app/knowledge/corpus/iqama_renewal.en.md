# Iqama Renewal

## Who Is Responsible
The company sponsors and renews the Iqama (residency permit) for its non-Saudi employees in KSA.
You do not need to visit a government office yourself; HR handles the renewal through the Muqeem
platform.

## Timing
Iqama renewal is initiated automatically before expiry. You will receive proactive reminders at
90, 60, 30, and 7 days before your Iqama expires, and so will your HR coordinator.

## What You Need to Do
Keep your passport valid for at least 6 months beyond the Iqama renewal date, and confirm your
current address and emergency contact with HR when asked -- this information is required for the
renewal filing.
