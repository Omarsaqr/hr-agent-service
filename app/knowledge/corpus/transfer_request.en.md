# Internal Transfer Request

## Eligibility
Employees who have completed at least 12 months in their current role may apply for an internal
transfer to another department, project, or work location. Employees still within their probation
period are not eligible.

## Process
Submit a transfer request through the HR portal naming the target department and role. Your
current manager and the receiving manager must both approve before HR processes the move. Approval
typically takes 5-10 working days.

## Effect on Leave and Tenure
A transfer does not reset your accrued leave balance or length-of-service tenure. Entitlement
continues to be calculated from the original hire date, unaffected by the move.
