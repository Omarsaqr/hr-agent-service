# Grievance Procedure

## Raising a Grievance
Employees may raise a workplace grievance -- about a manager decision, a colleague's conduct, or a
policy application -- through the HR portal or directly with an HR coordinator. Grievances can be
submitted anonymously, though anonymous grievances may be harder to investigate fully.

## What Happens Next
HR acknowledges every grievance within 2 working days and aims to conclude an investigation within
15 working days. You will be informed of the outcome even if some investigation details must stay
confidential to protect other parties.

## Escalation
If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may request review by a more senior HR manager
within 10 working days of being notified of the original decision.
